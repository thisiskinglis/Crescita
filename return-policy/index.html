Drop 001 — 100 pieces is FINAL SALE. No change-of-mind returns or refunds. This does not affect your rights for faulty goods under the Consumer Protection Act.

Size Exchanges Only

Got the wrong size? We will exchange for another size if we still have it, within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, with all original tags attached and in original packaging. No deodorant marks, perfume or smoke.

Exchanges are subject to availability. If your size is sold out, we will offer you a refund.

Faulty or Damaged

If your piece arrives faulty or damaged, email hello@crescitaofficial.com within 48 hours of delivery with photos and your order #. We will arrange a free collection and replacement or full refund. You have rights under the CPA for 6 months for manufacturing defects.

How To Start An Exchange

1. Email hello@crescitaofficial.com with order # and reason
2. We reply with return address / PUDO slip within 24h
3. Customer pays return courier — same as Yaga — unless faulty. Faulty = we pay.
4. Refunds / exchanges processed 3-5 business days after we receive and inspect the item.

Refunds

Refunds go to original payment method. Shipping fees are non-refundable, unless the item is faulty.
