Shipping Policy

CRESCITA is cut & sewn in SA, shipped from Johannesburg.

Processing Time

All Drop 001 orders are limited stock. Allow 2-5 business days processing before dispatch. You will receive a tracking number / drop-off code via email / WhatsApp once your order is sent.

How Shipping Works — Same As Yaga

You pay for shipping at checkout. We don't add it into the set price. You choose how you want it sent, we drop it, you track it.

Domestic (South Africa) — Buyer Pays

Choose one at checkout:

  • PUDO Locker-to-Locker — cheapest
    Drop at PUDO locker, collect at your nearest locker anytime.
    2-4 business days after dispatch.
  • PAXI — PEP to PEP
    Drop at PEP, collect at your PEP.
    2-4 business days after dispatch.
  • The Courier Guy — Door-to-Door
    We book a home pickup, they deliver to your door.
    2-4 business days after dispatch. Delivery 9am-5pm weekdays.

Shipping is calculated at checkout based on which option you pick. We will confirm your option + code on WhatsApp before we send.

International

3-8 business days via DHL / Aramex. Duties and taxes are customer's responsibility and not included. We'll confirm your shipping quote on WhatsApp before you pay.

Lost / Delayed Parcels

Once handed to our courier, we are not liable for delays due to courier, weather or customs, but we will open a claim and assist you fully. If a parcel is marked delivered but not received, you have 72 hours to notify us.

Address Errors

Please double-check your address / locker. Re-delivery fees for incorrect addresses / lockers will be charged to customer.
