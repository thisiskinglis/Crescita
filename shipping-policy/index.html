Shipping Policy

CRESCITA is cut & sewn in SA, shipped from Johannesburg.

Processing Time

All Drop 001 orders are made to order / limited stock. Allow 2-5 business days processing before dispatch. You will receive a tracking number via email.

Domestic (South Africa)

Courier Guy / PUDO: 2-4 business days after dispatch. R99 flat rate, FREE over R1000. Delivery 9am-5pm weekdays.

International

3-8 business days via DHL / Aramex. Duties and taxes are customer's responsibility and not included. Calculated at checkout where possible.

Lost / Delayed Parcels

Once handed to our courier, we are not liable for delays due to courier, weather or customs, but we will open a claim and assist you fully. If a parcel is marked delivered but not received, you have 72 hours to notify us.

Address Errors

Please double-check your address. Re-delivery fees for incorrect addresses will be charged to customer.
